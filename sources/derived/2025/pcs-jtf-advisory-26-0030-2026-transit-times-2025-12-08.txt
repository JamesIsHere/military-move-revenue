PCS JTF Personal Property Advisory #26-0030

Date: 08 December 2025

From: Permanent Change of Station Joint Task Force (PCS JTF) Defense Personal Property Management Office
(DPMO), Scott AFB, IL 62225

To: All Military Service Headquarters Representatives, Worldwide Personal Property Shipping Offices
(PPSOs), Personal Property Processing Offices (PPPOs), and DoD-Approved Personal Property
Transportation Service Providers (TSPs)

Subject: 2026 USTC Domestic - International Transit Time Tables

1. New Special Solicitation (SS) Groups Class 1, COS 4 CONUS to/from HMAS Stirling, Western
Australia (AS26) and COS 8 CONUS to/from HMAS Stirling, Western Australia (AS26) transit times are
being updated for the 2026 DP3 Tender of Service rate year. No adjustments are being made to other
codes of service (see new attachment).

2. The updated transit time tables will be posted to the USTRANSCOM DP3 Personal Property
Website at https://www.ustranscom.mil/dp3/pdfs.cfm under the DP3 Business Rules Regulations
and Forms tab.

3. Corresponding new SS to Code 4 and Code 8 transit times in the Defense Personal Property System
(DPS) updated 5 Dec 2025 and will be accomplished for shipments with a desired pickup date on or
after 15 May 2026. Please send questions and concerns reference this advisory to the Operations Team
at transcom.scott.tcj9.mbx.pp- ops@mail.mil

4. This message is approved for release by the Chief of Operations, Defense Personal Property
Management Office, TCJ9-O.
