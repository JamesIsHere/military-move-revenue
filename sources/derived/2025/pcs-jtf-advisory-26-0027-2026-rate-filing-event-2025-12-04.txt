Subject: 2026 Defense Personal Property Program (DP3) Rate Filing Event Notice

5. Rates filed during this event will cover the period of 15 May 2026 - 14 May 2027.

6. Filing Rates in DPS:
   6.1. Using Workbench:
         6.1.4. Error and Rate Rejection Codes: Descriptions are available in the DPS Rate Filing
                User Guide (TSP Edition).

7. Bid Component Data Retention Process:
   7.1. TSPs filing bids for Domestic channels must file four (4) complete bid components. All
        four (4) components must be accepted by the end of Rd2 for the bids to be considered a
        valid discount for that Channel/COS:
         7.1.1. Peak Linehaul
         7.1.2. Peak SIT
         7.1.3. Non-Peak Linehaul
         7.1.4. Non-Peak SIT

9. TSP's Responsibilities:
   9.1. TSPs, or their representatives, are responsible for computing their own rates and must be
        familiar with the 2026 business rules: 400NG, 400NG Baseline Rates, International Tender,
        Tender of Service, and Claims and Liability Business Rules, which can be found at
        https://www.ustranscom.mil/dp3/pdfs.cfm.
   9.2. The latest TSP Rate Filing User Guide can be found in the Rate Filing Workbench (when
        Rd1 opens) by clicking on the "Rates Workbench User Guide" link. The User Guide
        provides details on functionality.

11. By filing DP3 Rates under this solicitation, TSPs agree to the following statement:
   11.1. For 2026, I understand the DoW will continue moving shipments utilizing DPS and
         Electronic Transportation Operational Personal Property Standard System (TOPS).
