Rules for Placing Shipments in SIT:

(1) Direct Delivery Address at Destination: For shipments with a direct delivery address at
destination (as indicated on the Bill of Lading) the TSP is not authorized to place a
shipment into SIT prior to a percentage (See the International Tender) of the
Government Transit Time (some exceptions may apply - e.g., member becomes
unavailable or unable to accept delivery). Subject to PPSO approval, the TSP may use
any DOD-approved SIT facility.
