# Request for Source-Backed DP3 Billing Clarification

**To:** [Contact name / firm]
**From:** [Your name / company]
**Date:** [Send date]
**Subject:** Domestic DP3 400NG billing sources and interpretation questions

We are developing a read-only post-audit process for completed domestic DP3
shipments. Its purpose is to reconstruct what a Transportation Service Provider
should have billed the Government and compare that result with invoice and
payment history. It does not submit invoices, move money, or address claims,
international shipments, NTS, DPM, or agent compensation.

We have reviewed the 2026 400NG tariff, 2026 rate workbooks, the 12 August 2022
Item Code Listing, current DTR material, and available PPA resources. Before we
extend the audit beyond narrowly approved Items 28A and 28B, we would appreciate
written guidance on the following three questions.

## 1. Current domestic Item Code Listing

Does the **Item Code Listing dated 12 August 2022** remain applicable to domestic
400NG shipments during the 2026 rate cycle (15 May 2026 through 14 May 2027)? If
it does not stand alone, what baseline, amendments, advisories, business rules,
or replacement artifact form the complete current contract?

The listing supplies fields that materially affect billing validation, including
item code, unit, rate-date basis, schedule/location, EDI instructions, approval
screen, and whether approval is required. We are particularly interested in
codes 226A, Item 28C, SIT families, Item 105, Item 120, and Item 130.

## 2. Date selecting SIT and accessorial rates

When original requested pickup and actual pickup fall in different rate cycles,
which date selects the applicable SIT or accessorial rate table?

- 400NG Item 1.2(c), page 18, states that covered SIT/accessorial services use
  tables in effect on the **actual pickup date**.
- The incorporated 2026 rate sheets display **“Based on Original Requested
  Pickup Date.”**

Please identify the controlling rule, any service-specific exceptions, and how
corrections are handled when the two dates select different tables. A boundary
example spanning 14–15 May would be especially helpful.

## 3. Reweigh-fee 5,000-pound branch

For 400NG Item 4.5, which weight determines whether a shipment is in the
“5,000 pounds or less” branch: initial net weight, reweigh net weight, the lower
net weight, a containerized provisional weight, or another accepted shipment
weight? Please also confirm the treatment at:

- exactly 5,000 pounds;
- exactly a 150-pound difference; and
- exactly a 5-percent difference.

Relatedly, please confirm whether the reweigh fee is billed through 226A with
the required “reweigh fee” note, rather than 4A/4B, and whether it is excluded
from discounting.

## Requested support

For each answer, the most useful support would be the governing document or
publisher clarification, its version and effective dates, and a section, page,
cell, or item-code locator. A sanitized or hypothetical billing example would
also help distinguish the governing rule from system or office practice.

Please do not send names, addresses, signatures, shipment numbers, government
identifiers, account information, or other live personal or financial data.
Any historical example should be authorized and sanitized before transmission.

If an answer reflects customary practice rather than published authority,
please label it as such. We will preserve competing interpretations and route
unresolved financial questions for human review rather than silently selecting
an outcome.
